# Spill Response (Non-Hazardous)

## 1. Scope
Covers food, beverage and water spills on hard floors and carpet in public areas. Chemical spills, blood or bodily fluids, and sewage are NOT covered here; for those, follow the chemical spill or biohazard procedures and notify Environmental Health & Safety.

## 2. Safety precautions
- Place wet-floor signs on all approaches before starting; spills in walkways are slip hazards.
- Wear gloves. If the liquid is unknown, smells chemical, or is near electrical equipment, stop and escalate.
- Do not mop around energized cords or floor boxes; unplug or have Electrical isolate first.

## 3. Tools & parts
- Wet-floor signs, absorbent pads or powder, dustpan.
- Mop and bucket with neutral floor cleaner; wet/dry vacuum for large volumes.
- Carpet extractor or spotter for carpeted areas; trash bags.

## 4. Procedure
1. Post wet-floor signs around the spill.
2. Pick up solids and broken glass with a dustpan (never by hand).
3. Absorb liquid with pads or absorbent powder; use the wet/dry vacuum for more than about a gallon.
4. Clean the area with neutral cleaner and rinse to remove sticky residue.
5. Dry the floor with a dry mop or floor fan.
6. For carpet, blot (do not rub), then use the extractor and leave a fan running.

## 5. Verification & closeout
- Floor is dry and not slippery or sticky; remove signs only when dry.
- If the spill came from a leak (ceiling, fixture, equipment), open a plumbing work order for the source.
- Record the location and approximate volume.
