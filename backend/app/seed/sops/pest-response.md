# Pest Sighting Response

## 1. Scope
Covers sightings of rodents, cockroaches, ants, bed bugs, stinging insects and birds inside buildings. Pesticide application is performed only by the licensed pest control contractor; campus staff handle sanitation, exclusion and monitoring.

## 2. Safety precautions
- Do not apply pesticides unless licensed. Do not handle dead rodents or droppings without gloves and a mask.
- Stinging insect nests near entrances: cone off the area and keep people away; do not spray.
- Food service areas must be inspected the same day to protect public health.

## 3. Tools & parts
- Gloves, N95 mask, disinfectant, sealed bags.
- Glue boards or snap traps (non-toxic monitoring devices only).
- Flashlight, door sweeps and sealant for gaps.

## 4. Procedure
1. Record the pest type, number seen, exact location and time of day.
2. Inspect for food sources, standing water, and entry points (gaps under doors, pipe penetrations).
3. Remove food debris and overflowing trash; fix leaks that provide water.
4. Place monitoring devices along walls near the sighting.
5. Seal small gaps with sealant and install door sweeps where light is visible under exterior doors.
6. Request a contractor visit for active infestations, food-service areas, bed bugs or stinging insects.

## 5. Verification & closeout
- Monitoring devices checked after 3 and 7 days; results recorded.
- Contractor service report attached if a visit occurred.
- Repeat sightings in the same area within 30 days are escalated for an exclusion project.
