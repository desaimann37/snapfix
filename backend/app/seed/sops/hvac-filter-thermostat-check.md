# HVAC Filter and Thermostat Check

## 1. Scope
First-response procedure for "too hot", "too cold", "no airflow" and noisy vent complaints in a single zone. Covers thermostat verification, supply/return airflow checks and filter replacement on air handlers (CoolAir CA-AHU-3 and similar). Building-wide temperature problems go to the controls technician.

## 2. Safety precautions
- Lock out the air handler before opening access panels; fans can start automatically.
- Wear a dust mask when changing heavily loaded filters.
- Watch for hot coils and steam piping in mechanical rooms.
- Do not adjust setpoints outside the campus range (68–76 °F) without approval from the HVAC lead.

## 3. Tools & parts
- Digital thermometer or IR thermometer, anemometer or airflow hood.
- Replacement filters (MERV 13, correct size listed on the unit tag).
- Thermostat batteries (if battery-backed), screwdriver set, flashlight.

## 4. Procedure
1. Measure room temperature at desk height and compare with the thermostat reading and setpoint.
2. Check the thermostat mode, schedule and occupancy override. Replace batteries if the display is blank or dim.
3. Feel or measure airflow at the supply diffusers. Little or no airflow means a closed damper, failed VAV box, or a clogged filter upstream.
4. Lock out the air handler, open the filter rack, and inspect filters. Replace any filter that is visibly loaded or past its change date.
5. Check the condensate pan for standing water and the drain for blockage; overflowing pans cause ceiling leaks below the unit.
6. For rattling or banging, check for loose diffuser covers, damper linkages and fan belts.
7. Restore power and confirm the unit starts and supply air temperature is within 5 °F of design.

## 5. Verification & closeout
- Room reaches setpoint within 45 minutes, or a follow-up controls ticket is opened.
- Filter change date written on the filter frame and in the work order.
- Record supply air temperature, room temperature and any dampers or belts adjusted.
