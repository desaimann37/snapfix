# Ceiling Tile Replacement

## 1. Scope
Covers stained, wet, broken, sagging or missing acoustic ceiling tiles in suspended grid ceilings. A water stain is a symptom: always find and stop the water source (roof, pipe, condensate pan, window) before replacing tiles, or the new tile will stain again. Several stained tiles across one wing or floor in a short period suggests a roof or riser leak, which should be inspected as one problem.

## 2. Safety precautions
- Wet tiles are heavy and can collapse without warning. Cone off the area below a sagging tile and never stand directly under it.
- Tiles in older buildings may contain asbestos. Check the building's asbestos survey before disturbing tiles marked "suspect"; if unknown, stop and contact Environmental Health & Safety.
- Check for live wiring and light fixtures resting on the grid before lifting tiles.
- Use a ladder rated for the task and keep three points of contact.

## 3. Tools & parts
- Matching replacement tiles (2x2 or 2x4; check edge profile and texture).
- Utility knife and straightedge for cutting border tiles.
- Moisture meter, flashlight, drop cloth, cones, plastic bags for wet tiles.

## 4. Procedure
1. Cone off the area. Photograph the damage and note the tile grid location.
2. Use the moisture meter to map wet tiles and look above the grid with a flashlight to locate the water source.
3. If the source is active, create or escalate a plumbing/roofing work order and place a bucket or tarp. Do not replace tiles until the leak is stopped.
4. Lift the damaged tile up and tilt it out of the grid. Bag wet tiles to avoid dripping.
5. Check the grid for bent tees; straighten or replace them.
6. Insert the new tile at an angle, then lower it flat into the grid. Cut border tiles to fit.

## 5. Verification & closeout
- Tiles sit flat with no gaps; grid is level.
- Moisture meter reads dry on surrounding tiles, or a follow-up check is scheduled within 48 hours.
- Record the water source found (or "not found") in the work order.
