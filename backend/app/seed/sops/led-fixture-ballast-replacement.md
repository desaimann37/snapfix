# Light Fixture Ballast / LED Driver Replacement

## 1. Scope
Covers troffer and linear fixtures (LumaTech LT-24/LT-40 and similar) that are out, flickering, humming or partially lit. Fluorescent fixtures are converted to LED during repair when the conversion kit is in stock. If several fixtures on the same circuit fail at once, check the breaker and lighting control before replacing components.

## 2. Safety precautions
- De-energize the circuit at the panel and apply lockout/tagout. Verify absence of voltage with a tested non-contact tester and a meter.
- Use a fiberglass ladder rated for the load; maintain three points of contact.
- Broken fluorescent tubes contain mercury: bag them in a sealed container and follow universal-waste rules.
- Do not work above occupants; cone off the area below the ladder.

## 3. Tools & parts
- Replacement LED driver matched to fixture wattage and dimming type (0–10 V for LT-24), or ballast for legacy fixtures.
- LED tubes or LED retrofit kit if converting.
- Insulated screwdrivers, wire strippers, wire nuts/push-in connectors, multimeter, non-contact voltage tester.
- Fiberglass ladder, cones, universal-waste container for tubes.

## 4. Procedure
1. Confirm the failure: check the wall switch, occupancy sensor and breaker. If the whole zone is out, troubleshoot the circuit first.
2. Lock out the circuit and verify zero voltage at the fixture.
3. Open the fixture lens/door and remove lamps or LED boards.
4. Photograph the existing wiring, then disconnect the driver/ballast leads.
5. Install the new driver, matching line, neutral, dimming and output leads exactly. Secure the driver to the fixture pan.
6. Reinstall lamps/boards and close the fixture.
7. Remove lockout, restore power, and test on/off and dimming.

## 5. Verification & closeout
- Fixture lights fully with no flicker or hum for 5 minutes.
- Dimming and occupancy sensor operate normally.
- Record the driver part number and fixture tag in the work order; dispose of tubes as universal waste.
