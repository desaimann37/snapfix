# Door Closer Adjustment

## 1. Scope
Covers surface-mounted hydraulic door closers (SecureClose SC-4 and similar) on doors that slam, will not latch, close too slowly, or drift open. Fire-rated doors must self-close and positively latch; a fire door that does not latch is a code issue and should be fixed the same day.

## 2. Safety precautions
- Do not remove the closer arm while the spring is under tension; follow the manufacturer's release sequence.
- Keep fingers clear of the hinge side and the arm pinch points.
- Never wedge or prop open a fire-rated door while working; stay with the door or post a fire watch.
- If the closer is leaking oil, replace it rather than adjusting; a leaking closer will fail.

## 3. Tools & parts
- Hex keys and screwdrivers matched to valve screws (usually 1/8 in hex or flat-blade).
- Replacement closer of the same size/grade if leaking or broken.
- Shims, hinge screws, and a level.

## 4. Procedure
1. Watch a full closing cycle. Identify the problem: slams at latch, closes too slowly, does not latch, or swings too fast through the sweep.
2. Check hinges and the strike first: tighten loose hinge screws and confirm the latch lines up with the strike plate.
3. Locate the valve screws: sweep speed (S) and latch speed (L). Some models also have backcheck (BC).
4. Adjust in small increments (1/8 turn). Clockwise slows, counter-clockwise speeds up. Do not back a valve screw out past the closer body; it can leak.
5. Target: the door closes from 90° to 10° in 3–6 seconds and latches firmly without slamming.
6. Adjust spring power if the door will not overcome air pressure (common at entrances and stairwells).

## 5. Verification & closeout
- Door closes and latches from full open five times in a row.
- Opening force does not exceed 5 lbf for interior doors (accessibility requirement).
- Record which valves were adjusted and any parts replaced.
